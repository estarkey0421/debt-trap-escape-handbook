# State Action Kits
### Per-state cheat sheets and template letters — companion to The Debt Trap Escape Handbook
**Second Edition (v1.3) — July 2026**

> **How to use:** find your state below (Tier 1 first, then Tier 2). Each kit = a 5-step cheat sheet + a fill-in-the-blank letter with your state's statute baked in. **Step 1 (verify) is not optional.** No kit for your state? It's Tier 3/4 — use the Handbook's Universal Playbook instead of a void-loan letter that isn't true. Bright line: these kits are for escaping debts the law says you don't owe; borrowing with intent not to repay is fraud.

**Verified universal contacts:** CFPB — consumerfinance.gov/complaint · 1-855-411-2372 | FTC — ReportFraud.ftc.gov · 1-877-382-4357 | License lookup — nmlsconsumeraccess.org | Legal aid — lawhelp.org | Consumer attorneys — consumeradvocates.org/find-an-attorney | Credit counseling — nfcc.org · 1-800-388-2227 | Military — base JAG · 1-800-342-9647

## Arizona — Tier 1 Kit (loan void)
**Key law:** A.R.S. § 6-613(B) · **Cap:** 36% + fees (small) · **Regulator:** AZ Dept of Insurance & Financial Institutions — difi.az.gov · ✅ cite verified July 2026

**Cheat sheet:**
1. **Verify (15 min):** type **A.R.S. § 6-613(B)** plus the word "law" into Google and click the result ending in **.gov** — that's the official copy; you're looking for one word: **"void"** (or "uncollectible" / "unenforceable"). Then check the lender's name at **nmlsconsumeraccess.org** and in the "License Lookup" at difi.az.gov — no results means unlicensed — and search difi.az.gov for a warning naming your lender. Confirm your loan's date and size fit the law and that you were in-state when you borrowed. Screenshot everything. **Confused at any point? Just call:** the phone number is on the Contact page at difi.az.gov; ask *"Is [LENDER] licensed in this state, and what does the law say about loans from unlicensed lenders?"* — they answer that for free every day.
2. **Bank first (20 min):** call the number on your debit card: *"I need a stop payment on all ACH debits and charges from [LENDER], and I am revoking my authorization. Please block all future debits."* Note date, time, rep's name.
3. **Send the letter (30 min):** fill every [BRACKET], sign, send certified mail w/ return receipt (~$9) AND email. Keep the receipt.
4. **File all four complaints (45 min):** (a) AZ Dept of Insurance & Financial Institutions — difi.az.gov → File a Complaint; (b) your state AG; (c) CFPB — consumerfinance.gov/complaint / 1-855-411-2372; (d) FTC — ReportFraud.ftc.gov. Attach the loan agreement.
5. **Stop paying:** the loan is void. Check credit at annualcreditreport.com in 30 days; dispute any tradeline. To claw back payments: lawhelp.org or consumeradvocates.org/find-an-attorney.

**Template letter:**

> [DATE]
> [LENDER LEGAL NAME], Attn: Legal / Compliance Department, [LENDER MAILING ADDRESS]
> *Sent by certified mail no. [TRACKING NUMBER] and by email to [LENDER SUPPORT EMAIL]*
>
> **Re: Loan No. [LOAN NUMBER] — [YOUR FULL NAME] — NOTICE OF VOID LOAN AND DEMAND TO CEASE ALL COLLECTION**
>
> To whom it may concern: This letter concerns the loan identified above, made on [LOAN DATE] in the amount of $[AMOUNT FINANCED] at a disclosed APR of [APR]%. I was a resident of, and physically located in, the State of Arizona when this loan was applied for and made, as your records reflect.
>
> **Under A.R.S. § 6-613(B),** a consumer lender loan made by a person who is not licensed under Arizona's Consumer Lenders chapter is void, and the lender has no right to collect or receive any principal, finance charges, or other fees.
>
> You hold no license from this state, and no license issued by any other body — including a tribal regulatory authority or a partner bank's charter — satisfies this state's licensing requirement or displaces its law. This loan is therefore void, and I owe you nothing on it. I demand that you and your agents, servicers, successors, and assigns: (1) immediately cease all collection activity, including electronic debits, calls, texts, emails, and letters; (2) close the account with a zero balance; (3) refund all amounts I have paid within thirty (30) days; and (4) furnish no negative information to any consumer reporting agency — this debt is disputed and void, and any furnishing will be disputed under the FCRA, 15 U.S.C. § 1681s-2, and reported to the CFPB.
>
> **Revocation of payment authorization.** Per the Electronic Fund Transfer Act and Regulation E, 12 C.F.R. § 1005.10(c), and any revocation clause in the loan agreement itself, I revoke, effective three (3) business days from the date of this letter, all authorizations to initiate ACH debits, remotely created checks, card charges, or other transfers from any account of mine. I have separately instructed my bank to block such debits; any later debit is unauthorized. I also revoke all consent to calls and texts to the fullest extent permitted by law, including the TCPA, 47 U.S.C. § 227 — communicate in writing only.
>
> **Regulatory complaints.** I have filed documented complaints with AZ Dept of Insurance & Financial Institutions, the state Attorney General, the CFPB, and the FTC. Any continued collection of this void debt will be forwarded to each agency and may itself be an unfair or deceptive practice (see *CFPB v. CashCall, Inc.*, No. 15-cv-7522 (C.D. Cal. 2016)) actionable under this state's consumer protection statutes.
>
> Nothing in this letter waives any right or remedy, all of which are expressly reserved. Govern yourselves accordingly.
>
> Sincerely, [YOUR FULL NAME] — [YOUR ADDRESS] | [YOUR PHONE] | [YOUR EMAIL]
