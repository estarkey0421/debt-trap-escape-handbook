# The Debt Trap Escape Handbook
**A free, state-by-state guide to your rights against unlicensed, tribal, and other predatory high-interest lenders — with pre-written letters for 29 states.**

If you took out an online loan at 200%, 400%, 600% APR from a "tribal" or unlicensed lender, there is a real chance **the law in your state says that debt is void — legally nonexistent** — and the lender is counting on you never finding that out.

**Read it here:** [The Handbook](Debt_Trap_Escape_Handbook.md) · [State Action Kits (29 states)](State_Action_Kits.md)

**What's inside:**
- A Quick Start page: six steps, about three hours of work, to stop the automatic debits and dispute the debt
- A 51-jurisdiction map: which states void these loans entirely, which void the interest, and which don't protect you (told honestly)
- Pre-written, statute-cited demand letters for 29 states — fill in the blanks, sign, mail
- The bluff decoder: every threat collectors use ("we'll sue," "you'll be arrested," "process server tomorrow") and the truth about each
- A verification protocol so you confirm the law yourself before acting — no trust required
- The one real danger (never ignore genuine court papers) and exactly what to do about it

**What this is not:** legal advice, a debt-settlement service, or a way to borrow money you don't intend to repay (that's fraud, and this guide says so on page one). It's public law — statutes, court cases, and regulator warnings — organized so a scared person at a kitchen table can actually use it. Every statute is cited; entries verified against primary sources are marked; everything tells you how to double-check it.
